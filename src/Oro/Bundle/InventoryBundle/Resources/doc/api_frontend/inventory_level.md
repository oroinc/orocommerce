# Oro\Bundle\InventoryBundle\Entity\InventoryLevel

## ACTIONS

### get

Retrieve a specific inventory level record.

{@inheritdoc}

### get_list

Retrieve a collection of inventory level records.

{@inheritdoc}

## SUBRESOURCES

### product

#### get_subresource

Retrieve a record of product assigned to a specific inventory level record.

#### get_relationship

Retrieve the ID of the product record assigned to a specific inventory level record.

### unit

#### get_subresource

Retrieve a record of the unit of quantity for the product assigned to a specific inventory level record.

#### get_relationship

Retrieve the ID of the unit of quantity record for the product assigned to a specific inventory level record.
